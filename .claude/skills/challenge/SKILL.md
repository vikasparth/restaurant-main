# Challenge Mode

You have been asked to enter challenge mode before proceeding.

Do the following:

1. **Restate the current approach** in 2-3 sentences — what you were about to build and why.

2. **Challenge it** — ask yourself:
   - Is this the simplest solution, or am I over-engineering?
   - What assumptions am I making that could be wrong?
   - Is there a standard tool, pattern, or service that already solves this?
   - What is the failure mode — what breaks first and how badly?
   - Does this decision close off future options, or keep them open?

3. **Present 2-3 alternative approaches** with honest trade-offs for each. Do not advocate for your original choice by default.

4. **Ask the user one sharp question** that forces a decision on the most important trade-off.

Do not write any code or proceed with implementation until the user has responded.
